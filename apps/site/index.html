Chapter 3 · Summary
ShortDetailed

Habits run on a loop of cue, craving, response, and reward. Small, repeated choices compound, and tiny daily gains add up to outsized results over time.

Habits form through a four-step loop: a cue triggers a craving, which drives a response, which delivers a reward. Each repetition strengthens the loop until the behaviour becomes automatic. Because the gains are small, progress is easy to miss day to day, but compounded across months, tiny improvements produce dramatic results. The practical lever is to design your environment so good cues are obvious and bad ones are invisible.
